## Purdue University — Daniels School of Business

---

### MGMT 306 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Management Science &nbsp;·&nbsp; Undergraduate

An introduction to quantitative modeling for managerial decisions, covering applications in pricing, production planning, project scheduling, and resource allocation.

---

### MGMT 472 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Advanced Spreadsheet Modeling and Simulation &nbsp;·&nbsp; Undergraduate

A course in building decision models in spreadsheets, combining optimization, simulation, and sensitivity analysis on realistic business problems.

---

### DSB 60110 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Industry Practicum &nbsp;·&nbsp; Master's

An industry practicum course for master's students.

---

### MGMT 590-037 &nbsp;·&nbsp; AI-Enhanced Optimization &nbsp;·&nbsp; Graduate, Summer 2026

This course covers nonlinear optimization, constrained optimization, and modern AI-driven decision-making techniques. Topics include gradient-based solvers, KKT conditions, stochastic gradient descent, global optimization, and Bayesian optimization — all implemented in Python.
